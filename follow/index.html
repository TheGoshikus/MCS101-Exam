The practical details, in plain English.

Still have a question?
info@follow.com.ai

What do I need to take part?

An iPhone and a health plan you want to follow. A watch or ring can add context, but neither is required.

Is early access free?

Yes. The four-week early access test is free.

When will the test start?

We’re planning the first invitations for December 2026. We’ll email you with the next steps.

What happens to my health data?

You choose what to upload and which sources to connect. You can disconnect them at any time. We do not sell your data, and you can delete your account and its data.

Does FOLLOW replace medical advice?

No. FOLLOW helps you organise and follow recommendations you already have. It does not diagnose conditions or replace advice from your clinician.

Can I take part on Android?

This first test is for iPhone. Android is not part of the initial test.

What if I stop using FOLLOW?

You can stop taking part. If you can share what got in the way, your feedback will help us improve FOLLOW.

Can I invite a friend?

Yes. Share this page so they can request early access too.
