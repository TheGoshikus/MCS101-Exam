FAQ

Questions

Is FOLLOW a medical app?

No. FOLLOW is a wellness app that helps you build everyday habits. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything and doesn’t replace your doctor.

What do I need to take part?

An iPhone. We start with Apple Health, so data from Apple Watch and apps that write to Apple Health comes in automatically. A watch or ring helps but isn’t required.

What happens to my data?

You choose what to connect and can disconnect it at any time. We never sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it whenever you want.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The test is free.

When does the test start?

We plan to send the first invitations in November 2026. Everyone on the list hears from us by email.

Do you support Android?

Not yet. Android comes after iOS. Join the list and tell us you’re on Android so we invite you first when it’s ready.

What if I stop using it?

That’s fine. Tell us why if you can; it helps more than you’d think.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. After you join you get a personal link. Friends who join with it move you up the list.
